Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy covers Triangle Battle Worker (the "App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Triangle Battle 2D YouTube channel, the @trianglebattle2d TikTok and Instagram accounts and the Triangle Battle 2D Facebook Page. The App is used only by the channel owner to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App requests these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish the channel's own videos to its own channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name and the statistics of the channel's own videos.
  • Google Drive (drive): to read and write the job files and video previews the App keeps in its own "Triangle Battle Pipeline" folder in the owner's Drive.

When the channel owner connects TikTok, the App requests these TikTok permissions:

  • user.info.basic: to show which TikTok account is connected.
  • video.upload and video.publish: to send the channel's own videos to the owner's TikTok account, either as drafts in the TikTok inbox or as posts.

When the channel owner connects Instagram and Facebook (Meta), the App requests these permissions:

  • pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management: to find the owner's own Facebook Page and the Instagram account linked to it.
  • pages_manage_posts: to publish the channel's own videos as Reels on the owner's own Facebook Page.
  • instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish: to show which Instagram account is connected and to publish the channel's own videos as Reels on it.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the channel's videos, to pass jobs between the owner's own automation tools, and to measure how the channel's own videos perform. The App does not access any other user's account or data.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube, TikTok and Meta

The App uses YouTube API Services, TikTok for Developers APIs and the Meta Graph API. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy, the TikTok Terms of Service, the TikTok Privacy Policy, the Meta Terms of Service and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and TikTok access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, and Meta access in Facebook under Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations. Deleting the tokens from the owner's computer also ends the App's access.

Deleting your data

The App keeps no data about anyone other than the channel owner. To delete everything it stored, remove the App's access as described above and delete the tokens and logs from the owner's computer, or email the contact address below with the subject "Delete my data" and it will be removed within 30 days.
